## The Big Picture: maNipulify

Neonify started as a side-project (like every HAKORA project — IMDER did, VODER did).
But it may get bigger later. The long-term idea:

```
        ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
        │               maN i p u l i f y             │
        │        "media manipulation, ify-style"      │
        │                                             │
        │   N stays as the logo                       │
        │   "ify" stays as the style/naming law       │
        │   name = media manipulation                 │
        └─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Neonify** keeps growing: gets **"cartoonify"** effects next to the neon ones
- Maybe integrating **asciidea** in it (ASCII rendering as another "ify" style)
- Maybe **Neonify becomes part of IMDER**, and then gets into **VODER's side quests**
- A refactor into something cooler, possibly called **maNipulify**:
  - the **N** remains as the logo
  - the **"ify"** remains as the style
  - the name becomes **media manipulation**
- Possibly an **add-on in IMDER's C++** as a compiled `.so`/`.dll`
  plus a **plugin for the Python one**
- **VODER as DLC** for the side quests
